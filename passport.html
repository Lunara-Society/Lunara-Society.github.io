    What the passport reads
    
    
    Three questions at the border
    
    
    
      
    
        
        
    Is the connection safe?
    
        
    HTTPS, HSTS, a content security policy and the headers that stop clickjacking and content sniffing.
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
    Can its name be forged?
    
        
    DNSSEC, SPF and DMARC decide whether someone else can send email or answer DNS as this domain. CAA limits who issues its certificates.
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
    Does it speak to machines?
    
        
    An llms.txt for AI systems, a declared policy for AI crawlers, and a security.txt so a researcher who finds a hole knows whom to tell.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    The score never decides the visa. A site can score 100 and still be not_registered: the register only stamps a domain after a named reviewer has checked who runs it. That separation is the point.
