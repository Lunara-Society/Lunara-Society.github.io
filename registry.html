Founding Participant Program

The founding cohort shapes the standard. Founding status is recorded in the register and does not expire on its own — but no entry here is permanent, because certification is revocable and revocations are published as openly as certifications. A register whose entries could never be withdrawn would not be worth querying. The businesses that establish a standard first carry a different weight than those who join after it exists.
