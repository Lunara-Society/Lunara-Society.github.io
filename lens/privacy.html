Privacy policy

Rosario (Lunara Lens), on the web and for Android (package com.lunarasociety.lens). Last updated 26 September 2026.

Rosario is made by Lunara Society (“we”). This page says, plainly, what the app collects, what never leaves your phone, who else handles your data and why, and how to delete everything. If anything here is unclear, write to lunarasociety@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Everything that can run on your phone does, and stays there: your memory, lists, notes, reminders, files, medical card, safe word and settings.
  • When you use Rosario’s AI, the photo, text or recording you chose is sent for that one answer. We do not keep it.
  • We keep your account, your credits and plan, and a log of which AI actions you used and what they cost, never their content.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train AI.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete my account) or from the web.

What is sent for an answer, and not kept

When you ask Rosario something that needs AI (checking a photo, video, voice, text or message for signs of AI or a scam, describing or reading what the camera sees, explaining a letter, translating, writing, planning, a question), the app sends what that answer needs: the image or video frames you chose, the text, measurements of a recording (not the recording itself) and any transcript, and your question. With a question it also sends what you asked Rosario to remember, your lists and whether you are in work or home mode, so she can use them.

This is processed by Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI models, to produce the answer. Lunara does not store it. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to be used to train their models; Anthropic may retain it for a limited time for safety and legal reasons.

With Pro and Luna Max, Rosario’s natural voice is made by ElevenLabs from the text of her reply. The text is sent to ElevenLabs for the audio and not kept by us.

What stays on your phone

  • What Rosario remembers, your lists, notes and reminders, and work or home mode
  • The files and photos you keep in the app, your medical card and your family safe word
  • Your settings and the conversation on screen
  • On-phone checks: file metadata, content credentials, error-level analysis, sound measurements, text recognition and the magnifier and colour finder run on your phone

Deleting the app, or your account from inside the app, removes them.

Permissions

  • Camera: only while a camera screen is open, to check, describe, read, scan or magnify what you point it at.
  • Microphone: when you tap to speak, turn on hands-free, or record a voice to check. Your phone’s speech service (on Android, Google’s) turns speech into text.
  • Location: only when you ask for the weather (the approximate coordinates go to the Open-Meteo weather service), share your location in an emergency, or save where you parked. It is not sent to Lunara.
  • Notifications: to remind you.
  • Google Drive (optional): only if you connect it, and only for files the app itself creates.

Payments

In the Android app from Google Play, purchases are made and processed by Google Play; we receive the order ID and purchase token to grant your credits. On the web, you pay by debit or credit card or with PayPal in PayPal’s secure checkout, which processes the payment; we receive the order and payment IDs to add your credits. We never see your card details.

Who else handles data

  • Supabase hosts our database and server functions.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website and the web app.
  • Anthropic produces AI answers; ElevenLabs produces Rosario’s natural voice.
  • Google for sign-in, Google Play payments, and optional Google Drive; PayPal for web payments.
  • Open-Meteo for the weather in your briefing.

They process data to provide their service to us and not for their own advertising. Some are in the United States; transfers rely on their standard contractual safeguards.

Your rights

You can see what we hold about you, correct it, take a copy, object, or have it deleted. Delete your account yourself in the app, or email lunarasociety@gmail.com for anything else; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Rosario is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). The database is not reachable from the app directly: only our server functions can read it, and sessions expire. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and, for anything that matters, the app tells you.
