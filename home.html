How An Entry Is Earned

A certificate you cannot lose
is not worth holding.

Four stages to enter the register. One path out of it, and that path is what gives the other four their meaning.

Stage 01
Identity

Who you are, proved against the domain you claim to control.

Stage 02
Declaration

What governs your systems, stated by you and recorded in your words.

Stage 03
Review

A human checks the declaration against the seven pillars. Not a form that always passes.

Stage 04
Public record

The entry goes live and anyone may query it, without an account or a key.

And the way out

An entry can be revoked when what was declared stops being true, and the revocation is published as openly as the certification was. Nobody has to take our word that the register is current, because the record shows what changed and when.

Revocable
at any stage

Most certifications are assessed once and held permanently, whatever the holder does afterwards. That is why they stop meaning anything.
