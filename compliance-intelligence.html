The Regulatory Reality

Two laws. Zero guidance.
One compliance vacuum.

EU AI Act — Article 50

Transparency obligations now enforced

Article 50 requires disclosure when interacting with AI systems. The official Code of Practice excludes Article 50(1) agent disclosure requirements. The dedicated compliance page returns a 404 error. The signatory list remains unpublished past its deadline.

Penalty: EUR 15M or 3% global turnover

California SB 942 — AI Transparency Act

Dual jurisdiction, zero coordination

SB 942 became operative the same day as EU Article 50. Requires manifest disclosure, embedded metadata, and free detection tools for providers with 1M+ monthly users. Technical standards remain unpublished. No cross-border coordination mechanism exists.

Penalty: California AG enforcement + statutory damages

Enterprises deploying AI agents after August 2 must self-interpret both regulations without official guidance. The risk is clear: over-compliance means cost bloat. Under-compliance means regulatory exposure.
