Source, summary, interpretation

Three kinds of text appear here and they are labelled apart. The source is the official instrument, linked for every row; Lunara does not reproduce it. The summary is Lunara's own short reading, written against that source. An AI answer, if you ask Rosario from the inspector, is generated by a model, is not sourced, and opens in the Intelligence workspace marked as such.

Which of these obligations reaches a particular deployment is a further question, answered by the applicability model on Does the law reach your AI?, which is marked interpretation for that reason.

How the corpus is kept

No automated monitor watches these instruments. The corpus is maintained by hand, each row checked against its primary source on the date shown, and every deploy refuses to publish if the corpus and the site disagree. Changes reach it through editorial review and through the Agent Network's Corpus watch mission, where a reported change needs an official source and the owner's approval. Every edit is in the public change history.
