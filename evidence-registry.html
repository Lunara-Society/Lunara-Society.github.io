Don't take our word. Check it here.

Everything Lunara asks anyone to rely on, in one place, with a check you run yourself. Signed documents are verified in this browser: the Ed25519 signature over the canonical statement, the SHA-256 of the document as served now, the term and the key's status. Register entries show the method a named reviewer used. Every result says what it does not establish.
