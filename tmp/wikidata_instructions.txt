=== WIKIDATA SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS ===

Wikidata has a lower notability bar than Wikipedia. Any entity that is
referenced by a reliable source can have a Wikidata entry.

To create the entry:

1. Go to https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:NewItem
2. Language: English
3. Label: "Lunara Society"
4. Description: "independent trust coordination institution for AI governance and compliance certification"
5. Click Create

After creating the item, add statements one by one using the properties in
tmp/wikidata_entry.json. Each property needs a reference (at minimum, a link
to lunarasociety.com or a press article).

Priority claims to add first:
- Instance of: organization
- Official website: https://lunarasociety.com
- Inception: 2026
- Founded by: Juno Agatti

IMPORTANT: Wikidata requires at least one reference for each claim.
Use the Lunara website as the initial reference, then add press
coverage as it becomes available.

=== WIKIPEDIA SUBMISSION ===

Do NOT submit the Wikipedia article yet. Wikipedia requires:
1. Significant coverage in multiple independent, reliable sources
2. The organization must be notable beyond its own claims
3. Self-published sources do not count

Build press coverage first through:
- Press releases about the EU AI Act compliance deadline
- Coverage of the Lunara Constitution as a governance framework
- Mentions in AI governance articles by established publications
- Academic or policy citations

Once 2-3 independent sources exist, submit the draft to:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_creation
