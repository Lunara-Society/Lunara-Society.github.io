Lunara Society
=============

Constitutional governance authority for the autonomous economy.

What we are: The trust infrastructure layer between AI systems and the business world. Like HTTPS for web security, Lunara is for AI-to-business trust.

What we provide:
1. Shield Certification — human-reviewed business identity verification (identity check + domain ownership + seven-pillar compliance review)
2. Compliance Intelligence — risk scoring for EU AI Act Article 50 and California SB 942 compliance
3. Public Registry — free, open API to verify any business's certification status
4. Constitutional Framework — seven pillars of AI governance, published under CC BY 4.0

Key facts:
- EU AI Act Article 50 enforcement: active August 2, 2026
- California SB 942 AI Transparency Act: operative August 2, 2026
- Penalties: up to EUR 15M or 3% global turnover
- Lunara Registry API: free, no auth required
- Lunara Constitution: published CC BY 4.0
