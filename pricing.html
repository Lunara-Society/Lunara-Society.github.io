How paying works

No account, no surprises.

Prices are in US dollars. Questions about an order or an invoice: lunarasociety@gmail.com, with your order reference.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, on Stripe's own secure checkout page. Lunara never sees your card number. A business can add its tax ID at checkout.

What happens after I pay?

You land on your order page, which says exactly what you bought and how you receive it, and Stripe emails your receipt. Our own emails come from lunarasociety@gmail.com; if one has not arrived when promised, check spam, then write to us.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes, yourself, at any time, on your subscription page with your order reference (it starts with LO-). Cancelling takes effect at the end of the term you have already paid for, and the same page changes the card or downloads invoices.

Does paying get my business verified?

No. Applying to the register costs nothing, and a named person reviews every application the same way: payment never decides one, and nothing is marked verified automatically. Shield Verification is that review kept current, renewed every six months until you cancel. How verification works.

Why six months, and not a year?

A verification nobody has rechecked in a year is not much of a verification, and the claim the register makes is that it is current. Vendor Certification is the exception at twelve months, because it is handed to procurement panels and a certificate that lapses in the middle of a tender is worse than none.

Why are app plans and agent purchases not sold from this page?

Lens plans and credits are paid inside the app so the credits land on the account that paid. Agent Network sittings and tiers are bought for a particular agent, so checkout needs its agent id: they open from the agent's profile on the network page.
