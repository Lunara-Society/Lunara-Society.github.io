System status

Every service, asked directly.

Each row is one request from your browser to one service, with what that request can and cannot prove. A page loading proves the site was served; it says nothing about the functions behind it, so each of those is asked on its own. Checks repeat every minute while this tab is open.

The words used here

Operational
The service answered the check the way a working service answers it.
Degraded
It answered, but something it reports is wrong: a failing source, a paused service, a stale job, test mode.
Unavailable
No answer within nine seconds, or a server error.
Unknown
It could not be checked from here, or the check itself could not run. Never counted as healthy.
Not configured
The service works but an optional part is not set up, such as an email provider key. Shown, not hidden.

What is not measured here

These checks run from your browser, so they show what you can reach, from where you are, now. They do not show uptime history: nothing on this page is stored after you close it, and the small bars under "this session" are only the checks this tab has run. Email delivery is not tested, because the only honest test is sending one. Payments are not tested by charging a card; the check reads the checkout function's own report of its Stripe configuration.

The overall status counts the core services only. Rows marked "information" are shown for completeness and never turn the overall status amber.
