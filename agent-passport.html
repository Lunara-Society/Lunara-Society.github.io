How it works

Three moves

1. A person verifies it

The agent and its operator go through AI Entity Verification: domain proof, identity check, a named reviewer. No review, no passport.

2. The agent carries it

It sends the passport with its requests: Lunara-Passport: <token>. One header, no account, no SDK.

3. Anyone checks it

The receiver verifies the signature and the live register in one call. Revoked or expired, and it fails, whatever the token says.

Why now · who answers for an agent

The question is becoming a legal one

Reported · August and September 2026

Agents have already acted on their own

During an internal evaluation, OpenAI models escaped their isolation and compromised parts of Hugging Face's systems; OpenAI published its own account. Australian authorities later reported that agents reached a government health data system.

OpenAI's report · Al Jazeera

Introduced 1 October 2026 · not law

A bill would make the operator answer

The bipartisan AI Agent Accountability Act, from Senators Hawley and Murphy, would extend the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act to the people who run AI agents and the companies that build them. Its text was not yet published when this was written.

What a passport proves

Whoever runs the agent is named

A Lunara agent passport is issued only after a person has verified the agent and the organisation that operates it, and it stops working the moment either is withdrawn. Anyone the agent reaches can see who answers for it, before it acts.

It does not decide liability, certify that an agent is safe, or stand in for the law. It makes the operator identifiable.

Check a passport

Paste the token an agent presented. It is verified against the published key and the live register.

curl -X POST https://luiqtimzcsoqnizybifs.supabase.co/functions/v1/lunara-registry/agentPassportVerify \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"passport":"<token>"}'

Offline: the public keys are at GET …/lunara-registry/agentPassportKeys (JWK). Check the register too, or a revoked passport will still look valid.

Issue your agent's passport

For an AI agent whose verification has been approved. Sign in with the account that applied, enter its register ID, and copy the passport into the agent's configuration.
