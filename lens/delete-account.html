Delete your Rosario account

Rosario (Lunara Lens) is made by Lunara Society. You can delete your account and its data yourself, at any time.

From the app or this website

  1. Open Rosario and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Go to Account, open Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Open Rosario to delete my account

By email

If you can’t sign in, email lunarasociety@gmail.com from the address of your account with the subject “Delete my Rosario account”. We confirm and delete within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Lunara account (email, name, Google ID, profile)
  • Your plan, credits (including unused credits) and credit history
  • The log of AI actions you used
  • On the phone you delete from: what Rosario remembers, your lists, notes, reminders, files and settings

What is kept, and why

  • References to payments you made (order and transaction IDs, the product), without your email address, so one payment can never be claimed twice and refunds can be handled.
  • Lunara Marks you created stay in the public register as revoked, with your name removed, so people checking a marked image see that it is no longer vouched for.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or it will keep charging. Web plans paid by card through Stripe never renew by themselves.
